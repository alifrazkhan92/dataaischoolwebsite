Our Team

Three dedicated professionals committed to delivering high-quality, regulated AI and data science education across the UK.

Ali Fraz Khan

CEO & Principal · Head of Centre

FHEA · PgCert HE · MCT · AZ-204 · AZ-400 · AZ-104

Ali is a Senior Lecturer in Computing with over eight years' experience in UK Higher Education. A Fellow of Advance HE and Microsoft Certified Trainer, he leads the school's academic delivery, quality assurance, safeguarding, and compliance. He is also the school's Data Protection Officer and Prevent Lead.

His teaching connects live industry experience, Azure, DevOps, AI engineering, directly to learner outcomes, ensuring every programme reflects what employers actually need.

Designated Safeguarding Lead Data Protection Officer Prevent Lead Compliance Manager
info@dataaischool.com

Khadija Khan

Lead IQA & Internal Verifier

Lead Internal Quality Assurer · Access Arrangements Coordinator

Khadija leads all internal quality assurance activity across the school's NCFE qualifications. She designs and maintains the IQA strategy, conducts sampling of every assessor each term, chairs all standardisation meetings, and maintains direct claim status across all programmes.

She is the primary contact for NCFE's External Quality Assurer during EQA visits, and reviews all recognition of prior learning and reasonable adjustment applications.

IQA Strategy Standardisation Lead Direct Claim Status RPL Decisions
info@dataaischool.com

Sheherbano Khan

Registrar & Examinations Officer

Learner Support Lead · Marketing Lead · Work Placement Coordinator

Sheherbano manages all aspects of learner registration, NCFE Portal administration, and certification claims. She is the first point of contact for learners on all administrative, support, and technical matters, and oversees the school's examination logistics.

She monitors learner engagement through weekly VLE analytics, coordinates all IAG sessions, and manages the school's marketing communications and work placement programme.

NCFE Portal Learner Support Examinations Marketing Lead
info@dataaischool.com
