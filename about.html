About The Data and AI School

We’re a London-based coding bootcamp focused on data and AI. Our mission is to help you go from beginner or generalist to job-ready specialist in Python, Data Science, AI and Agentic AI.

Our mission

To close the gap between traditional education and what the industry actually needs. We teach the tools, frameworks and practices used by data and AI teams today, so you can contribute from day one.
