Policies

The policies that govern how The Data and AI School of London operates as an NCFE registered centre (centre approval in progress), covering quality and assessment, learner support and safeguarding, and governance and compliance.

Our policies are reviewed regularly and applied across everything we do. A full copy of any policy is available on request, please email info@dataaischool.com.
