AI Strategy and Roadmap

Many organisations know they need AI but do not know where to start. We work with your leadership team to identify where AI delivers the fastest return, then produce a prioritised, costed roadmap you can execute immediately.

  • Use-case identification ranked by impact and feasibility
  • Build vs buy vs integrate recommendations
  • 12-month execution roadmap with milestones
  • Board-ready presentation and executive summary

02 / Automation

Business Process Automation

Repetitive manual work kills productivity and introduces errors. We identify your highest-cost workflows and replace them with AI-powered automation that runs reliably, 24 hours a day.

  • Document processing: invoices, contracts, forms, PDFs
  • Email triage, classification and auto-response drafting
  • Data extraction and validation pipelines
  • Automated reporting and dashboard population
  • CRM, ERP and third-party API integrations
  • Built on Python, n8n, Make or your existing stack
