DAIS-POL-019
Staff Recruitment, Induction, and CPD Policy
Data and AI School of London — NCFE Approved Centre

Policy Owner: Head of Centre
Version: 1.0 | Date: April 2026 | Review: April 2027
NCFE Risk Level: Medium
Mode of Delivery: Fully Online

19.1 Purpose and Scope

This policy ensures that all staff involved in the delivery, assessment, and quality assurance of NCFE qualifications are appropriately recruited, qualified, inducted, and supported through ongoing CPD, including skills specific to online teaching and assessment. All recruitment, induction, and CPD activities are conducted remotely.

19.2 Safer Recruitment

  • A written job description and person specification is produced for every role.

  • All applications are reviewed against objective criteria.

  • Interviews are conducted via video conference and include safeguarding-related questions.

  • All successful candidates provide proof of identity and right to work in the UK.

  • An Enhanced DBS check is obtained for all staff with learner contact before they begin.

  • A minimum of two professional references are obtained before appointment.

A Single Central Register (SCR) of all pre-employment checks is maintained and available to NCFE upon request.

19.3 Staff Roles and Minimum Qualifications

Role Minimum Qualification
Online Tutor / Assessor Relevant sector qualification; assessor award (TAQA/A1/CAVA) or working towards one
Internal Quality Assurer (IQA) IQA award (TAQA/V1/IQA) or working towards one
Examinations Officer Training in NCFE Portal administration and JCQ/NCFE exams officer guidance
Online Invigilator NCFE/JCQ online invigilation training
Designated Safeguarding Lead Level 3 DSL qualification; online safeguarding training
Head of Centre Relevant management experience; knowledge of NCFE compliance requirements

19.4 Online Induction

All new staff complete an online induction within their first two weeks, covering:

  • The VLE and all digital tools used in delivery

  • NCFE Centre Agreement obligations and key policies

  • Online safeguarding and the DSL's contact details

  • Data protection and GDPR in a remote context

  • Online assessment integrity and malpractice prevention

  • Digital accessibility requirements

  • Communication standards and boundaries with learners

Completion of induction is recorded on the staff portal.

19.5 Continuing Professional Development (CPD)

All delivery and assessment staff undertake a minimum of 30 hours of CPD per year, which must include:

  • At least 5 hours related to online teaching and digital assessment practice

  • Any updates to the NCFE qualifications they deliver

  • Annual refreshers in safeguarding, data protection, and equality

  • Attendance at NCFE standardisation and administration events

CPD is recorded on the staff portal and reviewed annually by the Head of Centre.

19.6 Performance and Development

All delivery staff have an annual appraisal conducted via video call, reviewing performance, identifying development needs, and setting objectives for the coming year. Where performance concerns are identified, a support and improvement plan is put in place.
