Quick answers about our qualifications and how we work.
What qualifications does The Data and AI School of London offer?
We offer NCFE regulated qualifications across five subject areas: Programming (Python, Java, C#, PHP), Cyber Security , Data Science and Analytics , Cloud and Digital Support , and Functional Skills English . Qualifications range from Entry Level to Level 5 on the Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF).
Are the qualifications regulated and recognised by employers?
Yes. All qualifications are regulated by NCFE, an Ofqual-approved Awarding Organisation. They sit on the Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF) and are nationally recognised by UK employers and universities.
Yes. All qualifications are delivered 100% online through our Moodle-based Virtual Learning Environment. You can study from anywhere in the UK with structured tutor support and flexible self-paced study.
How are the qualifications assessed?
Most qualifications use portfolio-based assessment, internally marked by our qualified assessors and verified by NCFE. Functional Skills English is internally assessed by our centre. There are no traditional written exams for most qualifications.
Do I need prior experience to apply?
Entry requirements vary by level. Level 2 qualifications are open to most learners aged 16 and over. Level 4 and 5 qualifications typically require prior learning at Level 3 or equivalent experience. We assess each applicant individually and recommend the most suitable qualification.
How do I apply and what happens next?
Fill in our short online application form . We will email you within two working days to confirm your place and discuss your study plan.
How can I contact The Data and AI School of London?
Call us on +44 207 0990 956 (Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm), email info@dataaischool.com , or use our contact form . We aim to reply within two working days.
